Design Summary

  • Monochrome only — white on dark, opacity and weight do all the work
  • Toggle — segmented control, active side gets slightly brighter background
  • Play button — circular, right-aligned, triggers animation
  • RNN animation — words highlight sequentially, previous fade, hidden state bar shrinks
  • Transformer animation — all words + connection lines appear at once
  • Connection lines — SVG, thicker/brighter for strong relationships (cat↔sat), subtle for filler words
  • Dark/light theme — respects blog's existing theme prop (inverts opacity values)
  • Responsive — words wrap or shrink on mobile
