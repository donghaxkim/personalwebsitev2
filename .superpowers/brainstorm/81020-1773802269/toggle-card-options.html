Toggle Card: RNN vs Transformer

How should the toggle card look and behave? Pick the layout that feels right.

A

Sentence-Focused

A sentence like "the cat sat on the mat" is displayed in the card. Toggle to RNN mode: words highlight one at a time left-to-right, and earlier words fade/blur as the "sticky note" gets overwritten — you can see information literally disappearing. Toggle to Transformer mode: all words light up simultaneously with connection lines between related words (cat↔sat, sat↔mat), everything stays visible.

ROUGH IDEA
the cat sat on the mat
↑ RNN: earlier words fading away
B

Pipeline Comparison

The card shows a horizontal pipeline of word "slots." Toggle to RNN mode: a single processing cursor moves left to right through slots sequentially, with a small "memory bar" that shrinks as it goes — visualizing the bottleneck. Toggle to Transformer mode: all slots process simultaneously with a grid of attention lines, and the memory bar stays full.

C

Dual View: Sentence + Speed

Combines both ideas. Top half: the sentence visualization (fading words vs. connected words). Bottom half: a small "processing speed" indicator — RNN shows a slow sequential bar filling left to right, Transformer shows all bars filling at once. Drives home both the information loss and the speed advantage in one card.

ROUGH IDEA
the cat sat on the mat
↑ RNN: sequential processing, one at a time
