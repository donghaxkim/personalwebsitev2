Animation Behavior

RNN mode — press play:

  • Words highlight one at a time, left to right (~400ms per word)
  • As each new word highlights, previous words fade in opacity
  • Hidden state bar resets then shrinks progressively
  • Final state: only last word fully visible, rest faded

Transformer mode — press play:

  • All words light up simultaneously
  • Connection lines appear instantly between all word pairs
  • Strong connections (cat↔sat, sat↔mat) are brighter/thicker
  • Weak connections (the↔on) are subtle

Toggle: Switching modes resets and auto-plays the new animation
