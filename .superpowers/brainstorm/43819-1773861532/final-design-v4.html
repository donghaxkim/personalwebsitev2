AttentionPipeline v4: Formula Only

Pure formula build-up with scroll-locked progression. No matrix, no clutter.

Step 1 — Dot Product
QKT
measures how relevant each token is to every other token
Step 2 — Scale
QKT / √dk
keeps scores stable so softmax produces a smooth distribution instead of spiking
Step 3 — Softmax
softmax(QKT / √dk)
converts scores into attention weights that sum to 1
Step 4 — Weighted Sum
softmax(QKT / √dk) V
blends all value vectors weighted by relevance — each token's final representation

The experience

  • Formula builds up incrementally as you scroll — each step adds a new piece
  • New piece glows blue, previous pieces fade to 25% opacity
  • One clean sentence below — no labels, no bullet points
  • Progress dots at the bottom
  • Scroll-locked via GSAP ScrollTrigger — page pins while you step through
  • After step 4, the full formula stays visible briefly, then scroll resumes
